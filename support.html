Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. If you are a TestFlight tester, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app. Not testing yet? Join the beta on TestFlight. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were doing.

Common questions

How do I add a source?

Open the Sources tab and tap +. Paste the address of a feed, or of a website that has one, and Leaf finds the feed. For ready-made choices, open Suggested sources: the list follows your device's country, and you can switch country in the menu.

A source says it couldn't load.

Publishers sometimes move or retire their feeds. Check that the address still works in a browser, then remove the source and add it again with the new address. A small warning sign shows next to a source that failed on the last refresh.

What does Leaf Plus include?

Leaf is free for up to 5 sources. Leaf Plus is an optional monthly or yearly subscription that removes the limit and adds notifications from sources you choose, a morning edition and saving stories to read later. Reading, search, the widget, import and export of sources and iCloud sync are free.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Cancel in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. In Leaf, Settings has Manage subscription and Restore purchases.

Why did a notification arrive late?

Leaf has no server. iOS decides when an app may check for news in the background, and it does so less often when the battery is low, Low Power Mode is on or you rarely open the app. Alerts therefore arrive some time after a story is published. Make sure Background App Refresh is on for Leaf in the Settings app.

How do I get a morning edition?

In Leaf, go to Settings, Notifications, and turn on Morning edition. Pick a time. The notification shows the top headlines from the last time Leaf refreshed, and tapping it opens the front page.

Will my sources and place carry over to a new phone?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud. Leaf syncs your sources, saved stories, reading place and read marks through iCloud, and they are also in your iCloud backup. After a restore, Leaf downloads the news again and opens on your page, or the nearest story if the old one has left the feed.

Can I bring my sources from another reader?

Export an OPML file from your other reader, then in Leaf open Sources, tap the … button and choose Import sources. Without Leaf Plus the import stops at 5 sources. To take your list with you, choose Export sources.

How do I jump to a story or find one?

Tap the contents button at the top of the Read tab. It lists the stories in the current section, and you can search all of your stories there. The menu at the top left lets you show one section or one source, or only unread stories.

How does Leaf work on iPhone Duo?

On the inner display Leaf shows two pages side by side. When the device is partly folded, the pages sit on either side of the fold and keep their text clear of it. The system moves the tab bar and toolbar to the side of the screen, as it does in other apps. The fold-aware layout needs iOS 27.1.

Can I change how the text looks?

Yes. In Settings, under Reading, you can choose typeface, text size and a white or sepia page.

Which languages does it support?

English and Swedish. It uses the language your device is set to.

What does Leaf know about me?

Nothing. There are no accounts and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
